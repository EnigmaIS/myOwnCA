How issuing-CA setup works

1 · The trust tree

Each certificate is signed by the one above it.

2 · Why an offline root + online sub-CA

The root is the crown jewel: a compromise means re-issuing everything. Keep it on an air-gapped machine and let an online sub-CA do day-to-day issuance. Test/Lab installs skip this and use a local self-signed issuer.

3 · The two keys

  • Root key — lives only offline; signs sub-CAs (and root CRLs). Never online.
  • Sub-CA key — generated and kept online; signs end-entity certs. Never offline.

4 · The ceremony flow

No private key ever crosses the gap — only the CSR goes out and the signed cert comes back.
